# NOVELIA – AI-Powered Global Reading Marketplace

**Discover stories. Connect with readers. Empower publishers.**

NOVELIA is an independent product prototype exploring an AI-powered global reading marketplace that connects readers and publishers through multilingual book discovery, personalised reading experiences, and intelligent assistance.

🌐 **[Explore the Live Prototype](https://novelia-ai-powered-global-reading-marketplace.ai.studio)**

---

## Project Overview

NOVELIA aims to create a connected reading ecosystem with two primary portals:

- **Reader Portal:** Discover books, explore reading experiences, manage favourites, and track reading progress.
- **Publisher Portal:** Manage books, monitor reader engagement, explore analytics, and manage promotional activities.
- **ROX AI Assistant:** An intelligent assistant concept designed to help readers discover books and support publishers with their workflows.

## Key Features

### Reader Experience
- Book discovery and exploration.
- Personalised reading experience.
- Reading progress tracking.
- Purchase and subscription concepts.
- Favourite books and reading dashboard.
- Chapter-based reader engagement.

### Publisher Experience
- Book management.
- Publisher dashboard and analytics.
- Earnings and payment information.
- Reader engagement insights.
- Promotional campaign requests.

### ROX – AI Assistant
- AI-assisted book discovery.
- Natural-language interactions.
- Reader and publisher-focused assistance.
- AI-powered workflow support.
